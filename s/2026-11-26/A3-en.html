A3. Country Focus (Germany): Hensoldt and Argus Interception expand C-UAS partnership, integrating A1-Falke net-capture drone into Elysion

German sensor prime Hensoldt and Argus Interception announced Nov 26 an expanded partnership integrating Argus's operationally fielded A1-Falke non-kinetic net-capture interceptor drone into Hensoldt's modular Elysion C-UAS C2 architecture.
