D8. Briefly noted (Country Focus — India): Indian Navy tenders Combat Management System maintenance for four Project 17A frigates

IDRW reported Nov 26 that the Indian Navy's Eastern Naval Command issued a multi-BOQ tender for 21–24 month Combat Management System CAMC covering four Project 17A frigates (Nilgiri, Udaygiri, Himgiri, Taragiri).
