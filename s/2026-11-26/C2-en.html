C2. Ukrainian Neptune cruise missiles strike Marinovka Su-34 airbase as long-range drones hit Bashkortostan oil pumping hub

Ukraine's General Staff confirmed Nov 26 that Ukrainian Navy coastal Neptune cruise missiles struck Russia's Marinovka Su-34 airbase in Volgograd Oblast overnight while long-range drones hit Transneft's Subkhankulovo oil pumping station over 1,000 km away in Bashkortostan.
