B5. Pentagon awards Pratt & Whitney up to USD 2.525B global F135 engine sustainment contract for F-35 fleet

NAVAIR awarded RTX's Pratt & Whitney a USD 2.525B contract running through November 2027 for global unit- and depot-level maintenance, spares, and software support of F135 engines across U.S., partner, and FMS F-35 operators.
