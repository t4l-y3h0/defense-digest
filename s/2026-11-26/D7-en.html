D7. Briefly noted: UKMTO Warning 222-26 reports projectile explosion near commercial oil tanker 50 NM west of Dubai

UKMTO Warning 222-26 and Anadolu Agency reported Nov 26 that an explosion from an unidentified projectile occurred close to an oil tanker ~50 NM west of Dubai at 21:30Z Nov 25, with crew safe and no structural damage reported.
