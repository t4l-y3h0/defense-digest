D4. Indian Navy's Project 28A Next-Generation Corvette program to outsource three of eight ships to private shipyards

Following the Nov 2 INR 53,866.50 crore (~USD 5.7B) Project 28A award to state-owned GRSE (5 ships) and GSL (3 ships), both yards are moving to outsource three corvettes (2 from GRSE, 1 from GSL) to private Indian shipyards.
