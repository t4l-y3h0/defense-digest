D12. Briefly noted (UPDATE): Struck LPG tanker TARA GAS still burning and adrift 5.7 nm off Oman

Briefly noted (UPDATE to Nov 18 D6): A NAVAREA IX navigational warning confirmed Panama-flagged LPG carrier TARA GAS (struck Nov 16 off Khasab; 1 crew missing) remained on fire and drifting 5.7 nm off Khawr Ghub Ali, Oman.
