D11. Briefly noted: UK Defence Minister pitches U.S.-built Babcock Arrowhead 140 frigate design for U.S. Navy

Briefly noted: During a Washington visit, UK Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry Luke Pollard pitched domestic U.S. construction of Babcock's Type 31 / Arrowhead 140 design to address the U.S. Navy's frigate shortfall.
