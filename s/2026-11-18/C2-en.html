C2. UPDATE: U.S. Air Force executes unarmed Minuteman III ICBM test launch (Glory Trip 257) from Vandenberg SFB

Air Force Global Strike Command executed the scheduled Glory Trip 257 launch of an unarmed LGM-30G Minuteman III ICBM from Vandenberg SFB at 23:01 PT Nov 17 (07:01Z Nov 18), sending mock re-entry vehicles ~4,200 miles to Kwajalein Atoll.
