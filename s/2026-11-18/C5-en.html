C5. UPDATE: Italian defense minister warns Leonardo on ICEYE's EUR 2.05B Avio bid as Avio board opens formal review

Italian Defence Minister Guido Crosetto urged state-backed Leonardo (18% Avio owner) on Nov 18 to 'think carefully before exiting Avio' after ICEYE's EUR 2.05B bid, while Leonardo's CEO said it will coordinate with Rome and Avio's board hired Jefferies and Chiomenti.
