C8. Saudi-led coalition strikes 60 targets including Houthi underground ballistic missile tunnels; Saudi defenses intercept cruise missiles

Saudi-led coalition forces struck 60 military targets across four Yemeni governorates on Nov 18—including mountain tunnels storing Houthi ballistic missiles and drone assembly sites—while Saudi air defenses intercepted retaliatory Houthi cruise missiles aimed at Riyadh.
