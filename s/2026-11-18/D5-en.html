D5. Briefly noted: UK Defence Minister confirms Devonport nuclear submarine dry dock (10 Dock / SWIF) upgrade is behind original 2027 schedule

UK Defence Minister Luke Pollard told Parliament on Nov 18 that the Submarine Waterfront Infrastructure Future (SWIF) / 10 Dock upgrade at HMNB Devonport faces schedule issues against its original 2027 delivery target, compounding RN attack-submarine maintenance queues.
