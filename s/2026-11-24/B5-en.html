B5. PLA Navy confirms Harbin Z-20F shipborne ASW helicopter will make public debut at Zhuhai Airshow 2026

Following yesterday's J-35/KJ-600 lineup news, the PLA Navy confirmed its 10-tonne Harbin Z-20F shipborne anti-submarine helicopter—designed to replace Z-9C and Russian Ka-28 fleets on Type 055/052D/054B warships—will debut publicly at Zhuhai (Dec 7–13).
