C2. Hellenic Air Force receives first Israeli Rafael SPICE kits and IAI/Elbit Rampage supersonic missiles for F-16 fleet

Greek City Times reports the Hellenic Air Force has received initial deliveries of Israeli Rafael SPICE-1000/2000 guidance kits and supersonic Rampage air-to-surface missiles under 2022 contracts, with F-16 integration entering its final phase.
