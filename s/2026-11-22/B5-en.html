B5. UPDATE: SpaceX Falcon 9 launches NROL-136 intelligence satellites on record 32h 3m Vandenberg pad turnaround

SpaceX launched the NROL-136 mission for the U.S. National Reconnaissance Office from Vandenberg SLC-4E at 12:23 UTC on Nov 22, setting an all-time SpaceX pad turnaround record of 32 hours, 3 minutes, and 21 seconds after USSF-317.
