B7. Briefly noted: Airbus Manching works council urges immediate German 6th-gen fighter start for 2032 flight, rejecting GCAP

At a Nov 23 assembly in Manching, Airbus Defence and Space's General Works Council urged Berlin to launch a German-led 6th-generation fighter targeting a 2032 first flight while opposing GCAP participation or additional F-35 buys.
