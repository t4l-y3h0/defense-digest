A3. Ukraine HUR confirms deep-strike destruction of rare Russian AP-MRIK-VKO aerospace surveillance complex in Smolensk

Ukraine's Defense Intelligence (HUR) confirmed Nov 25 that long-range strike drones destroyed Russia's AP-MRIK-VKO multifunctional aerospace reconnaissance complex in Smolensk Oblast, a rare sensor capable of tracking stealth aircraft and cruise missiles out to 2,500 km.
