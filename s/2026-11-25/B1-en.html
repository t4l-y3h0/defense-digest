B1. Israel MoD sets USD 20 billion 2026 defense export target as DECA adds 200 new exporters and 400 products

Marking DECA's 20th anniversary on Nov 25, MoD DG Amir Baram announced a target to surpass USD 20 billion in 2026 Israeli defense exports while Deputy Head Dov Sadeh disclosed ~1,300 active exporters (+200 in 2026), ~400 new products, and 5,900 export licenses.
