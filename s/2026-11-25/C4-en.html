C4. ISW/CTP assess IRGC developing hybrid ballistic missiles pairing Haj Qassem propulsion with MaRV warheads

Iran International reported Nov 25 on a new ISW/CTP assessment finding Iran's IRGC is combining Haj Qassem MRBM propulsion stages with maneuvering reentry vehicles (MaRVs) from Kheibar Shekan and Fattah-1—and exploring cluster payloads—to counter missile defenses.
