D3. Indonesian Navy takes delivery of first missile-capable 58-knot KSR X33 optionally crewed attack boat Patkamla Geledek

North Sea Boats delivered the Indonesian Navy's first 20-meter KSR X33 optionally crewed missile attack craft (Patkamla Geledek), featuring a 58-knot carbon-fiber hull and two anti-ship missile stations.
