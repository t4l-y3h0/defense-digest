B10. Briefly noted: USAF Air National Guard cuts half of TACP airstrike-control teams; Louisiana 122d squadron converts to cyber operations

Executing an FY25 USAF cut of 50% of Air National Guard TACP missions, Louisiana's 122d Air Support Operations Squadron redesignated on Nov 7 as the 122d Cyberspace Operations Squadron, targeting FOC in spring 2030.
