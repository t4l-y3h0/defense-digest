B9. Briefly noted (UPDATE): ULA Vulcan Flight 5 failure tied to vehicle fuel supply to BE-4 engines, tightening Space Force NSSL launch capacity

Air & Space Forces Magazine reported on Nov 19 that the Nov 15 Vulcan Centaur explosion—destroying 40 Amazon LEO satellites—stemmed from inadequate vehicle fuel flow to the BE-4 engines, leaving SpaceX Falcon 9 near capacity for Space Force NSSL missions.
